Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

The short version

Terrain shows public statistics on a map. It has no accounts, no analytics, and never asks for your location.

1. Who we are

Terrain is published by Sylvester Assiamah. Reach us through GitHub issues.

2. Information we collect

None. The app downloads a static dataset from GitHub Pages and Apple Maps tiles from Apple. Neither request carries anything about you beyond what any web request carries.

3. What stays on your phone

A cached copy of the dataset and your last chosen filter. Deleted with the app.

4. Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Updates to this policy will be posted at this address with a new date.
